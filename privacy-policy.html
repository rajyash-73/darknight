DarkNight Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. Applies to the DarkNight app for Android and the DarkNight extension for Firefox.

The short version

DarkNight doesn’t collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your themes, colors, font settings, your on/off choices for websites, and the date your free trial started are saved on your phone only. If you use Android’s backup, Android may include them in your own Google backup, which we can’t access.

How the app and Firefox talk

The app shares your current theme with the DarkNight extension through a connection that stays inside your phone (the “loopback” address 127.0.0.1). Nothing is sent over the internet, and other devices can’t reach it.

Websites you visit

The extension changes how pages look inside Firefox. It doesn’t read, record or send the content of pages or the addresses of sites you visit. The list of sites where you turned theming off is saved in Firefox on your phone.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We don’t receive your payment details; the app only learns from Google Play whether you’ve bought lifetime access.

Analytics, ads and tracking

DarkNight has no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party SDKs other than Google Play Billing.

Children

DarkNight isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
